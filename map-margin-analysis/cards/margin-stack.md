# Margin Stack   (live)

**Source:** table `tblEXAMPLE0000007`.
**What it is:** the chain from net revenue to the number the business actually watches — Final Projected Margin — plus the traffic-light alert.
**Shape:** `Gross Margin $ (Pre Soft Costs)` (`fldEXAMPLE0000035` = net revenue minus the hand-typed `Total Costs`; base A of cards/cost-stack.md) → minus `Soft Costs $ (Total)` (`fldEXAMPLE0000071` = net revenue × `Soft Costs %` `fldEXAMPLE0000072`, non-zero in 128 of 347 records) → `Gross Margin $ (Post Soft Costs)` (`fldEXAMPLE0000073`) and `Mid Margin % (Post Soft Costs)` (`fldEXAMPLE0000074`) → minus `Overhead and Fees $` (`fldEXAMPLE0000075` = net revenue × the overhead percent `fldEXAMPLE0000076`) → `Final Projected Margin $` (`fldEXAMPLE0000077`) and `Final Projected Margin %` (`fldEXAMPLE0000078`). The `Margin Comparison Alert` (`fldEXAMPLE0000070`) grades active rows only: red under 10%, yellow under 15%, healthy above.
**Hits:** `Soft Costs %` and the overhead percent are the two manual levers — each moves everything downstream of it and can flip the alert colour. So does any edit to base-A costs or to the revenue stack, since both ends of the subtraction come from there.
**Does not hit:** `Marketing and Admin $` (`fldEXAMPLE0000079`) and `Financing Fees $` (`fldEXAMPLE0000080`) — each filled in 3 of 347 records — roll into `Total Overhead/Admin/Financing` (`fldEXAMPLE0000081`), and that total feeds nothing. Final Projected Margin subtracts only `Overhead and Fees $`; filling the marketing or financing boxes changes no margin number. The alert also ignores `Final Projected Margin %` — it reads `Mid Margin % (Post Soft Costs)`.
